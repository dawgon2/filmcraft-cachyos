- **2026-10-08, session 4: Phase 3.2 JKL (done, synthetic, `docs/fork-benchmarks.md` "Phase 3.2").**
  No leftover changes; no `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`. Before: L 2× 50/189, L 4× 2/238, J 1× 44/76,
  J 2× 38/202 (quick). Causes found and fixed:
  - **Above 1× playback asked for every frame**: the frame due moved one timeline frame per
    refresh, so prefetch (key + i·step) covered every frame and 2× needed 60 fps of decoding.
    Now the playhead shows a fixed **skimming grid** (`SkimControl`, `FrameServer::playback_frame`):
    every `step`-th frame, starting at the speed's natural step, climbing a ladder (2, 3, 4, 5, 6,
    8, 10, 12, 15, 20, …) when 3 grid frames in a row are late (two rungs when 6 of 8 are), back
    down after 48 calm frames with frames ahead ready (doubled each time a step down had to be
    undone), remembered per speed. Monitor, multicam, meter and bench use the same grid.
  - **Skimming decode** (`cancel::with_skim`, `GopCache::frame_skim`): any picture within half
    a step will do. Every non-reference picture except the target is left out; a non-reference
    target is replaced by a reference neighbour; a sync sample within the tolerance is preferred
    (a leading picture of an open GOP otherwise costs the whole GOP before it); a later GOP is
    reached by a seek; a frame the decoder already passed (another worker asked for a later one
    first) gets the nearest decoded frame within a grid step instead of a seek back; the target
    is flushed out when the next grid frame lies in another GOP. Off-grid jobs are dropped when
    the step changes.
  - **Reverse**: a cache hit waited for the decoder lock while another job decoded the GOP
    before (≈1 s): decoded frames now have their own lock (`Store`), hits never wait. The cache
    keeps a GOP's worth while playing backwards (detected from the requests), evicts frames
    already handed out first, and one run decodes the whole display range (through the next sync
    sample and its leading pictures), flushed at its end. Reverse prefetch 40 frames, preroll 30
    frames / 2 s. The plan cache evicts frames playback has passed first (LRU evicted the
    prefetched frames still to be shown).
  - **GPU stall while the video engine runs flat out (driver behaviour, measured):** decoding a
    GOP back to back made the compositing pass wait 40–100 ms per frame (composite 0.4 ms, the
    rest `device.poll`), ~25 drops in 300; forward playback (spread decoding) never showed it.

    surfaces: no change), not the VA queue depth (1, 2, 3, 64 in flight: 12–44 drops). Pacing
